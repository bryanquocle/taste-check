# Rhodes 1991 MSG survey: extraction (propose only)

Source: Rhodes J, Titherley AC, Norman JA, Wood R, Lord DW. 1991. A survey of the monosodium glutamate content of foods and an estimation of the dietary intake of monosodium glutamate. Food Addit Contam 8(5):663-672. PDF: Papers\1991_Rhodes_FoodAdditContam_MSG+Content+UK+Foods+Survey.pdf. Table 1 values are in rhodes_table1.csv.

## Basis
- UK supermarkets (north-west England). **Only products that declare MSG** were sampled.
- Method: AOAC 1984 (ion exchange plus formol titration). It measures **total free glutamic acid** (natural plus added), reported as % MSG. That matches our free-glutamate column.
- The hydrate form isn't stated in the paper. MSG.H2O is assumed (AOAC 970.37 convention), so mg Glu = % x 1000 x 147.13/187.13. If it were anhydrous, values would be 7% higher.
- Recovery was 88.4%, and values weren't corrected for it.

## Recommendation
Apply only the onion soup mix fill: 2,972 mg/100 g, tier 3. Condensed soups (259, floor) are optional. Everything else is held, because the label-declared sampling doesn't carry over to plain US products.
